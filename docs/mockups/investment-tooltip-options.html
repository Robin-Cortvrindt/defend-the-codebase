Investments: three tooltip styles

Each version uses the same compact cards (name, pips, buy button). Hover any card to try it. One card is pinned open as an example. The levels you own are sample data.

1 · Dark popover beside the card

Matches the game's navy panels instead of a white box. A coloured edge shows the group. Opens to the side, so it never covers the cards below.

2 · Details strip under the list

No floating box at all: one fixed strip at the bottom of the screen shows whichever card you hover. Nothing gets covered, and the text can be bigger.

3 · Card unfolds in place

The hovered card grows to show the explanation, plus small chips for now / next level. The cards below slide down.
