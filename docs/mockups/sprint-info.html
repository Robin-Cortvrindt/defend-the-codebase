Sprint info: what's unclear and how to fix it

Today the only sprint info during play is one HUD stat, Y1 · Q1 · 3 / 7. It doesn't explain what the 7 means, how far you are into the running sprint, that a Security Risk boss ends every sprint, or that the quarterly review shop opens after sprint 7. Below are four ideas. They can be mixed: A is the minimum, B–D add more on top.

My pick

A + B + D1

A fixes the confusing label at almost no cost. B answers the question you have most during play ("how much longer?"). D1 uses the pause you already get between sprints to show what's coming. C is the richest but takes vertical room from the field; D2 is nice polish but optional.
