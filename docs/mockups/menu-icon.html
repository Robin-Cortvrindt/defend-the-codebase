Bug-Farm: main menu icon, 5 options

Each option sits left of the "Bug-Farm" title at 56 px, between the blue dash and the title. On the right: the icon at 128 px, plus 32 px and 16 px to check it still reads small. The same icon could also be the browser-tab favicon. Colours come only from the game palette (developer blue, orange and fuchsia, plus the drink green).
